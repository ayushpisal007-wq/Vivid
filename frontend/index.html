How it works

  1. 01 ▸ 01A

    Upload

    Your photo is turned upright and resized to at most 1024 px. The whole frame stays in: no square crop.

  2. 02 ▸ 02A

    Encode

    The generator shrinks the photo step by step. It keeps what is in the picture and where, and lets go of exact pixels.

  3. 03 ▸ 03A

    Stylize

    The generator repaints the picture as a cartoon. During training, a discriminator kept judging: real cartoon, or generated? Each got better by trying to beat the other.

  4. 04 ▸ 04A

    Compare

    You see before and after, four measured numbers, and the same photo through a plain filter with no AI.

photo domain↔cartoon domain

No one has drawn the cartoon twin of every photo. So the network learns from two separate piles: real photos and real cartoon frames, with no matching pairs. This is unpaired image-to-image translation, the same family as CycleGAN.

Q&A

How is this different from a photo filter?

A filter applies one fixed recipe to every pixel of every photo. The GAN learned what to do: it treats sky, skin and hard edges differently because it responds to what is in the picture.

You can see it on the fjord. The filter draws black dashes across the clouds, because it can't tell a cloud edge from an outline. The filter does have one advantage: it is predictable and never bends a face.

See AI vs Filter live

Where does it break down?

Faces can crack, fine detail gets smeared into blotches, and video would flicker because every frame is drawn from scratch. CartoonGAN also shifts colours, like a blue sky turning green.

We show these failures on purpose, using the same models you can run above.
